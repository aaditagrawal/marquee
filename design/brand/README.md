# Marquee brand kit

One colour on one ground: ink `#0B0B0C` and paper `#FAFAF8`. Everything is
generated by `build_brand.py` from the geometry in `mark.py`, so the favicon,
the README logo, the social card and the Android launcher icon and TV banner
are the same drawing. Regenerate from the repository root:

```sh
uv run --no-project --with cairosvg --with fonttools --with uharfbuzz \
    --with shapely --with pillow python design/brand/build_brand.py
```

`--kit-only` leaves the app resources alone.

## The mark

A round emblem called "reel lattice". A sea of overlapping film reels is drawn
like seigaiha waves: each reel is wound with bands of film around a windowed
hub, and each row hides part of the row behind it. An empty reel rises over
them as the sun, under an arc of sign bulbs. Nothing is outlined; the gaps
between the shapes do the drawing.

## Usage notes

- **Small sizes use the reel alone.** The full mark holds from about 48 px.
  At 16 to 32 px the favicon is the single reel, which is the same drawing as
  the sun and the hubs.
- **One colour only.** Ink on light grounds, paper on dark grounds. No
  gradients, tints or second colour. On a coloured ground, use paper.
- **Keep the gaps open.** Do not add an outline or a ring around the mark, and
  leave clear space of at least one bulb-arc width around it.
- Maskable icons keep the mark inside the central 80% safe circle.
- Wordmark: Hanken Grotesk SemiBold, tracking -2.5%.
