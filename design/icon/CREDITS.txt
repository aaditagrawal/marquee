Marquee mark, launcher icon and Android TV banner ("reel lattice")

What it is
  A round emblem in one colour, ink #0B0B0C on paper #FAFAF8 or the reverse.
  A sea of overlapping film reels is drawn like seigaiha waves: each reel is
  wound with bands of film around a windowed hub, and each row hides part of
  the row behind it. An empty reel rises over them as the sun, under an arc of
  sign bulbs. Nothing is outlined; the gaps between the shapes do the drawing.
  At 16 to 32 px the reel is used alone. The wordmark "Marquee" is set in
  Hanken Grotesk SemiBold.

How it was made
  design/brand/mark.py holds the geometry. design/brand/build_brand.py turns it
  into the Android VectorDrawables, the favicon set, logo lockups and social
  cards. The design and scripts were produced in November 2026 with an AI
  assistant (Anthropic Claude) at the project owner's request, through several
  rounds of studies, then reviewed and adopted by the owner as Marquee's
  identity. The owner supplied two monochrome emblems as references for the
  kind of look wanted (a round wave emblem and a banded sphere); nothing was
  traced or copied from them. Seigaiha, the overlapping-arc wave pattern, is a
  traditional motif in the public domain.
  It replaces the "folded light" M (October 2026, made with Claude Opus 5.5).
  design/icon/build_icons.py is kept as the shared drawing library (scene model,
  VectorDrawable and SVG writers, wordmark outlining) and can still render the
  old mark for reference; it no longer writes app resources by default.

Regenerate (from the repository root):
  uv run --no-project --with cairosvg --with fonttools --with uharfbuzz \
      --with shapely --with pillow python design/brand/build_brand.py
  Add --kit-only to leave app resources alone.
